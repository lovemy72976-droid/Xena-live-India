Community Guidelines

Be respectful. No harassment, threats, hate, sexual exploitation, child sexual content, non-consensual intimate material, scams, impersonation, illegal activity, spam or copyright abuse.

Report and block

Use the in-app Report and Block controls when you see prohibited content or abusive users. Reports may be reviewed by moderators and can lead to content removal or account restrictions.

Live safety

Hosts are responsible for moderating their rooms. Do not expose private personal information or encourage dangerous activity.
